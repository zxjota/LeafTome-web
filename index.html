This Privacy Policy describes how LeafTome (“we”, “our”, or “us”) collects, uses, and protects your information when you use our mobile application and related services. LeafTome is designed to help you thoughtfully capture, organize, and revisit your personal knowledge and reflections.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect the following types of information:

  • Device Information: such as device model, operating system version, language, and non-identifiable device identifiers.
  • Usage Data: interactions with app features, navigation patterns, crash reports, and performance metrics.
  • User Content: entries, notes, tags, collections, and preferences you create within LeafTome.

2. How We Use Your Information

Your information is used to:

  • Comply with applicable laws and regulations.

3. Data Storage & Security

LeafTome prioritizes your privacy. By default, your data is stored locally on your device. If you enable optional sync or backup features, data is encrypted both in transit and at rest using industry-standard protocols. We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to safeguard your information.

4. Data Sharing & Disclosure

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. Limited sharing may occur only in these circumstances:

  • With trusted service providers who support app functionality under strict confidentiality agreements;
  • When required by law, legal process, or valid governmental requests;
  • To protect the rights, safety, and security of LeafTome, our users, or the public.

5. Children’s Privacy

LeafTome is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe we have inadvertently collected such data, please contact us, and we will promptly delete it.

LeafTome may contain links or integrations with third-party services. This Privacy Policy does not apply to those services. We encourage you to review their privacy policies independently.
